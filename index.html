Case & Death Information

The Murder of 2 month old Mahogany White.

  • Decedent: Mahogany White.
  • Death: Mahogany White's death was ruled a non-accidental homicide due to blunt force trauma. Alexander Justice Wattley is Mahogany's biological father and he committed an act of filicide.
  • Autopsy: Wattley was initally charged with child cruelty due to admitting to dropping Mahogany not only once on the night that she died but also on 2 diffrent occasions that he told no one about. Once the autopsy was completed Wattley's charges were changed to murder & malice murder. The autopsy included fractured ribs and bleeding on the brain.
